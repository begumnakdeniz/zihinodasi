Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how the Zihin Odası application ("the App") handles data.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by the owner of the Zihin Odası TikTok account to publish their own videos. It does not have other users.

2. Data we access

With the owner's authorization, the App receives an access token from TikTok and reads basic account information (such as display name and available privacy options) that is needed to publish a video. The App does not access followers, messages, or any data belonging to other TikTok users.

3. How data is used

Access tokens are used only to upload and publish videos to the owner's account. They are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared or sold.

4. Data retention

The App does not store personal data beyond the authorization tokens required to operate. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services).

5. Third parties

Video content is generated with third-party AI services. No TikTok user data is sent to these services.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. Changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

For questions, contact the account owner through the Zihin Odası TikTok account.
